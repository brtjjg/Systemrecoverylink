### Complete Invoice, Payment Enforcement and System Recovery Website The website will be a complete billing, payment verification, subscription enforcement and recovery platform for your business. Its main purpose is to ensure customers pay their invoices on time, provide a clear payment process, automatically suspend access to your service when the deadline expires, and offer a controlled recovery process. The system will use your selected colours: deep black, yellow, green and red. ### 1\. Countdown and deadline enforcement The countdown is connected to the invoice deadline. ### PAYMENT COUNTDOWN ### 02 DAYS ### 08 HOURS ### 35 MINUTES Payment window active Illustrative countdown only. Countdown behaviour: * Green: sufficient payment time remains. * Yellow: deadline approaching. * Red: deadline reached or passed. * Zero-day grace period: enforcement begins immediately when the deadline expires. The server, not the customer's browser, determines whether the invoice deadline has passed. ### 5\. Recovery System before expiry Before the deadline, the customer can select RECOVERY SYSTEM. A popup explains the additional recovery fee of KSh 10,000. It clearly explains that this is an additional charge and does not clear the original invoice. For example: | Description | Amount | | ----------------------- | ---------- | | Original invoice | KSh 8,500 | | Additional recovery fee | KSh 10,000 | | Total required | KSh 18,500 | The customer sees the payment instructions, business Till number and recovery reference. The customer must be informed that paying the recovery fee alone does not settle the invoice or guarantee restoration. ### 6\. What happens when the countdown expires? Customer management and service enforcement Each customer will have an account or customer record with: * Customer ID. * Name and contact information. * Service or subscription ID. * Invoice history. * Payment history. * Deadline and enforcement status. * Recovery requests. * Suspension and restoration history. The system will enforce access through your own application's backend. For example, your application checks whether a customer's subscription is active before granting access to paid features. It will never delete customer records simply because an invoice is overdue. ### 10\. Database and technical architecture I recommend the following structure: | Component | Technology | | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | | Frontend | HTML, CSS, JavaScript | | Backend | Python Flask | | Database | PostgreSQL | | PDF invoices | Python PDF generation | | Payment verification | Verified provider integration or admin review | | Authentication | Secure admin login | | Hosting | Production web hosting with HTTPS | | Enforcement | Server-side subscription checks | | Notifications | Email and optional SMS integration | Important database records: * Customers * Services * Invoices * Invoice items * Payments * Recovery requests * Recovery payments * Subscription status * Enforcement events * Admin users * Audit logs The application should use a persistent database, secure administrator access, encrypted connections, backups, and authenticated payment notifications. ### 11\. Full customer journey * Invoice created Administrator creates an invoice and assigns a customer and deadline. * Customer receives invoice link Customer opens the payment portal. * Payment period begins Countdown is displayed and the recovery option is available. * Payment deadline expires If the invoice remains unpaid, the covered service is suspended immediately. * Recovery payment requested Customer sees the KSh 10,000 additional fee and outstanding invoice separately. * Payment verification Both payments are independently checked. * Service restored Backend reactivates the subscription after all required payments are verified. * Confirmation issued Customer receives confirmation and updated payment records. ### 12\. Final project scope The complete platform will have four main parts: * Customer portal: Invoices, payment instructions, countdown, recovery and payment status. * Payment engine: Payment records, verification, duplicate protection and receipts. * Enforcement engine: Deadline monitoring, immediate suspension and subscription restoration. * Administrator portal: Invoice creation, customer management, financial reports and recovery controls. One important business-policy point: Because the KSh 10,000 is an additional fee, it should be explicitly disclosed and agreed to by the customer before they accept recovery. The system should also allow you to configure whether an unpaid invoice can be settled normally without the recovery fee, or whether the additional fee is required under a clearly agreed recovery policy. This is the full specification I would use to build the actual website, rather than just a visual payment reminder.
